# Gospel Talks by Ori (this project's style)

Soft, coastal, calm. Ori's own brand sheet: Gospel Talks by Ori. The videos are in English, conversational faith
talks to camera in a bright room. Say it plainly, in a voice you would use out loud: one bold line, one italic turn,
one small label.

## Type (the headline formula: wide caps + one italic word)
- Headlines: Archivo Expanded Black, caps (fx/look/ArchivoExpanded-900.ttf, token --font-wide)
- Emphasis word: Instrument Serif Italic, one word or phrase per moment (the pack's caption face, --font-italic)
- Labels: JetBrains Mono, caps, spaced (.3em): kickers, verse references, "NEXT EPISODE ON"
- Body and captions: Archivo (captions at 600 for video legibility)

## Colour
- Canvas #EFE8D8: leads; the main ground for cards and the caption pill
- Breeze #CED8E2 and Mist #C6D2CE: soft grounds and panels
- Lagoon #8A9E9F: shapes and circles only (rings, checks, rules, the pulse and voice lines); never text
- Marine #6D7AA4: the hero colour, used once per screen: the one italic word that matters. Light text goes on
  Marine only at large sizes. Never Marine text on Mist or Breeze (too little contrast); on Canvas it is fine large.
- Slate #555E6D: all text, arrows, strikes, the dark ground
- Logo: Slate on light grounds, Canvas on Slate or Marine. The show name is set like the logo: "Gospel" in
  Instrument Serif Italic beside "Talks" in Archivo ExtraBold, BY ORI under it.

## Footage
Clean and cool-neutral (whites read white, no yellow cast), the room softly blurred behind Ori (a shallow depth of
field) while she stays sharp. Type behind the head is Slate and Marine on the bright wall, never on a coloured ground.

## Hi-fi
Wall type above the head in the formula (wide caps line, then the italic word). Captions in a Canvas pill, Archivo
in Slate, the *word* turning italic in Marine; thesis words are one big italic word in Marine. Cards on Canvas,
Breeze or Mist with Canvas panels; no graph paper, no gradients, no shadows, corners 6px.

## Never
- More than one Marine element on screen; Marine small text; Lagoon text.
- Highlighter swipes, glows, neon, emoji, stock religious clip-art.
- Made-up quotes or references: only Ori's words and plain true labels (DSM-5, Matthew 7:3-5).
